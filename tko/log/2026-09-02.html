2026-09-02 (0.0006849315068 years a slave)

We start walking through the water up to the gap through the wall in the North. Fitz gets his foot caught in a hole and feels something brush up on him and only freaks out a lot. We make it to the opening without too much trouble though. We see crumbling arcades, empty plazas, bats overhead, and streets with no water but filled with rubble. We get back to the Yuan-ti hole and Steve casts Tremorsense and feels 15 snakes around us. We are aware that the snakes can turn into Yuan-ti but if they are Yuan-ti they're in snake form. We head to the door and we see seven Yuan-ti. Six Broodguards and one Yuan-ti Malison named Nahth. Nahth says we can go in but as his prisoner. He mentions that their leader (Ras Nsi) is getting weaker and Fenthaza, a friend of Nahth, has a better vision of the future. We would be introduced to her after a few days to not raise suspicion. Ras Nsi is trying to raise Dendar, the Night Serpent. He's now trying to work for/with Acererak. We opt to go in as prisoners and strip out of our armor and weapons. Jack acts like he's just talking but starts to try to cast Charm Person on the guard taking his stuff. Nahth notices this and says he will chain Jack himself. They end up chaining all of our wrist and ankles and we're told to lay low for a day or two. Nahth tells us what we're going to want to go looking for when the time comes. He mentions our gear will be in another room, probably the store room. If it's inventoried we will have to find it.

As we walk in we see three pits with gates above them. They are used for defense if people storm the doors as they're filled with snakes. As we enter the room we see a hallway to the East, we also see a hallway that opens to a giant room with a snake statue to the South. We are led East and we see a door leading to the North as we pass it and we enter into a sacrificial room. In the room are overflowing jars filled with wet bones. We keep going East to the prison pits. We get chained to a wall in the prison pits and thankfully there is faint torch light. Nahth says we'll meet Fenthaaza but he's not sure how that will look. Could be one at a time or all that once and it could be a day or two. Yormash prays to Zou and talks about how this is all new to hiim. He has the Oath of Vengeance but he's learning when and how that righteous anger should come out. He asks for a sign that leading his friends into this situation isn't dumb and that he hasn't sealed their fate. After praying he feels a sense of calm. He relays that to the group and suggests that Zou feels we're on the right path. An indeterminate amount of time (1:37:52, thanks Fitz's Keen Mind) later a few guards enter and unshackle us from the wall. They put blindfolds on us but lead us somewhere we don't know.

As we enter we see a campfire in the cavern, muddy floors covered with footprints, and Nahth is staring in a hand mirror. Issar is the name of our overseer doling out tasks. Kota (his Chultan slave) tells us our jobs. Yormash will go to the dino pens and will be cleaning those. Jack will be cleaning the Yuan-ti nests. Steve will be scrubbing the Yuan-ti bathing area. And Fitz will be with Yormash. We get shuffled back out and we start with the dino pen. It's the same room with the giant snake statue we saw when we entered. Nahth is the leader there. Tells us to stay in the room but to get cleaning. Jack is taken to a room that was a catacomb. 18 alcoves and 12 are occupied. He's told to clean unoccupied alcoves. Jack hears Yuan-ti whisper to another in common "White feather, black mask." Steve is in a room with bloody pools in the room. Steve asks if he could take a bath if he does a good job and gets told he must attend to someone's every need. The Yuan-ti in the bath says they heard Fenthaza had a prophetic dream about Black Opal Crown which can usher forth Dendar the Night Serpent. We finish our jobs and go back to the holding cells. Jack and Steve tell us what they heard.

The next day Yormash has to clean the Yuan-ti nest, Jack helps the Xopal in poison distillery, Steve feeds the dead prisoner to the Hydra, and Fitz heads back to the dino pen. Xopal tells Jack that he is such an exquisite specimen. He tells him to go down to the water and pick fungi for his poisons. Steve is forced to drag a dead prisoner to the Hydra and throw the dead body in the water. They both seem to be near water but neither can see each other or know that the other is around. A piece of paper falls out of the dead body's pants (not known now but this is a crudely drawn map). Steve sneaks it in his hands and hears a Hydra approaching as he is leaving. Jack leaves the area with water and goes to the distillery with the fungi samples as it sounds like a giant beast is tearing through flesh.

We sleep and go into day three. We're getting anxious that we're going to be slaves much longer than we initially thought. We aren't blindfolded this time as we leave the prisoner pit. Fenthaza requests an audience with us after we finish our work today. Yormash goes to the storeroom, Jack goes to the bathing area, Steve goes to the Xopal, and Fitz goes with Steve to Xopal as well. Azi Mas is in the storeroom and tells Yormash to get to work organizing items as he walks in. Yormash sees stacks of wood crates, cloth, clay jars, etc. Yormash also notices a crate that is next to Azi Mas and he's preventing people from going near that crate, mostly keeping us on the other side of the room. He talks to Yormash about how Fenthaza seems shady and asks if Yormash has ever heard of her or anyone mention her. Yormash tells him that he's never heard anything and Azi Mas says I'll be his spy and to tell him everything I heard over the next 24 hours. As Steve and Fitz show up to the distillery, Xopal mentions someone will need to go get fungi for him. Steve just says "I volunteer" and is sent off to get fungi for poison. Fitz stays to help around the distillery but was told he's not a good stature for experiments like the rabbit the day before. We go back to our cell and eventually we're all taken to Fenthaza. She talks us through the plan to get our weapons and offers to change us into purebloods permanently. That would allow us to easily get into the Armory and to get through the tunnels. We decide we're going to stay in our original forms. Fenthaza says we're going to free the slaves after getting our gear and we're going to start an uprising. The Armory Password is "White Feather" before the door, "Black Mask" to the guard after the door is opened. The Armory is South of the giant snake statue, door on the right in the corridor. Storage is next to the assignment area (which Yormash knows as he worked there).
