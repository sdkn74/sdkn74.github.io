2026-10-07 (TYMORA DOES NOT BLESS)

After looting Xopal's distillery, Fitz finds 20 doses of Serpent Venom, 5 Essence of Ether, and 5 Essence of Topor. We head to the job assignment area to find some prisoners to arm but only find 2 Malison Yuan-ti and a Giant Boa Constrictor. Jack's wand spurs forth 600 butterflies and blinds the room for 10 minutes. We eventually kill them and move on to find the prisoners. We try to be stealthy but don't succeed. We find a room with a fire, 13 prisoners, and a Yuan-ti guard. The guard runs and Jack follows. Steve, Fitz, and Yormash arm the prisoners and 5 of the 13 decide to stay and help free others. Fitz gives aid and Yormash heals where he can.
