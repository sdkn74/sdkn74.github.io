2026-07-15 (Beware beware, the King of Feathers)

We start off with a long rest. 1st shift notices Dragonbait smelling like violets. He's afraid of the spiders. An hour into the rest they disappear and he seems much more comfortable. Rest of the shift is uneventful. 2nd shift notices rustling outside the light's circle but don't see anything. The rest of the shift is uneventful. 3rd shift Fitz hears rustling and sees a familiar Kobold (Kaka). We awaken to a sunny day. We start to head up the riverbank and Kaka's dead body is on the ground almost cut in half. Fitz and Jack see the King of Feathers closing in on us and we get about one round to prepare......FIGHT!

We finish killing the King of Feathers and pull Artus out of his mouth. Yormash heals him and runs towards Kaka who has nothing on his body. Fitz find the gold in the the King of Feathers. We put all the info we can into Volo's Guide and make a viable entry. Jack takes the head but Fitz takes some teeth first. Dragonbait smells of Honeysuckle and Ham (tenderness and concern)
