2026-11-04 (Kill Bitch Vol. 2)

Jack searches Ras Nsi and finds the Flame Tongue Longsword. We went back to the dinosaur area to fight, and as we start going to the blood pool, Jack notices a sad looking triceratops. He uses his Ring of Animal Friendship to cast Animal Friendship and Speak with Animals. Jack tells the triceratops to go to the "big open" and to get Starleaf. The triceratops says his name is Lamp (he sounds pretty dumb). Fitz looks over Ras Nsi not believing he only had a sword on him. He finds 600 gold pieces, a diamond, and 200 gold pieces worth of miscellaneous gems. We head to where we last saw Fenthaza.

As we head to the throne room, there's not much there. We notice there's a blue triangle behind the hydra looking iron throne and a disc on the floor. Fitz decides to go investigate the throne, nothing going on with it. Jack investigates the water and he determines it connects to where Jack chased a guy earlier in the dungeon. Yormash starts investigating the disc and notices it's a teleportation stone, but he's not sure where it goes. Fitz has seen a couple of these throughout the dungeon. Steve feels stuff behind the triangle using Tremorsense. Fitz sees a door seam in the wall behind the throne and Jack pushes it open. We see a muscular, full blood Yuan-ti and four Ghouls. We beat all four Ghouls and the Yuan-ti runs onto a teleporter disc and gets away. Yormash tried to break the teleporter to make sure he couldn't come back, but fails.

We take a short rest and during that Yormash and Fitz realize we're in a treasure room (the room behind the throne). Fitz finds the following

  • A Sembian wine flask (50 GP)
  • A bejeweled Amnian doublet with money pouch (75 GP)
  • A Cormyran cloak (100 GP)
  • A gilded Moon-shae harp (250 GP)
  • 3 silk cushions (25 GP each)
  • 150 platinum (1500 GP)
  • Ras Nsi leather spellbook (Priceless, for everything else there's MC)

We go back to get Ras Nsi head and we end up finding Bracers of Defense on Ras Nsi. Steve takes and equips them. We wait five hours for anyone to come by in the throne room. Yormash sees the shitty Yuan-ti guy again, runs down the hall yelling "The bitch is back!" We kill him.

Rewards and experience.

  • 1500xp for the forgotten name of the named Yuan-ti (31225xp total)
