2026-07-29 (SHOPPING TRIP!)

We head north and Jack sees a group of Su-Monsters. He uses the Wand of Wonder which casts Fireball. We end up fighting them and beating them and continue on our way. Jack jumps on his axe Bob to look at buildings in the water. Yormash grabs Fitz and flies up with both of them but sees nothing while Fitz finds the temple. We head back to tell the group while Jack finds a lot of waterlogged buildings. We use rope to cross the river with Jack using Bob to bring the rope around to the other side. Yormash is on the original side anchoring in case anything goes wrong. Everyone easily makes it over. We all start stealthing around the city but Yormash sucks and does not stealth well, thankfully there aren't any enemies or monsters to fight. We do make it to the Southern entrance of the circle city. We decide to go around the outside towards the cliffs.

We keep stealthing and Fitz notices a shack with a torchlight on it up against the canyon wall. We approach it and find it's magical shimmering drapes. We either have to go in or completely ignore it. Fitz knows it's illusionary magic which oddly looks very similar to buildings close by. Fitz knocks on the side of the building next to the drapes saying we're weary travelers and slowly opens the door. Apu of Apu's Arcane calls out to come on in. This looks like a foreign place with electrical candles in tubes. We find a 7-11 of magical shops! This is our shopping trip. P.S. Don't forget, attune to your stuff and we will come back and finish the shopping at the start of our next session.
